Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 September 2026 · Provider: Mike Sila

Summary

Launchlane helps B2B onboarding teams track customer onboarding in a shared workspace. To do that it needs an account, stores your workspace in our hosted database, and uses Apple for subscriptions. If you choose to use Copilot, relevant workspace records are sent to an AI provider to generate a suggestion. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across apps.

Information we collect

  • Account: your email address, and a password only if one was set up for your account (for example, an App Review account). Everyday sign-in uses one-time email codes.
  • Workspace content you enter: workspace names and membership, customer names, onboarding types, milestones, blockers, evidence, interventions, outcomes and notes, including drafts of customer notes.
  • Subscription records from Apple: product, status, renewal and expiry dates, transaction identifiers and whether the purchase was made in Apple's test environment. Apple processes the payment; we never receive card details.
  • Copilot usage: the time and type of each Copilot request, used to enforce usage limits.
  • On your device: a cache of your workspace for offline use and your sign-in session in the iOS Keychain.

We don't request contacts, location, photos, or microphone access, and this version includes no advertising, third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

How we use it

To sign you in, control who can access each workspace, keep your devices in sync, verify your subscription, answer Copilot requests you make, and enforce usage limits.

Copilot (AI suggestions)

Copilot asks for your permission before it shares anything, and you can withdraw it anytime in Settings → Copilot. It runs only when you ask it to. Launchlane then sends the relevant workspace records — and, for Capture, the note you typed — to our AI provider, Anthropic, PBC, to generate the answer. Before sending, Launchlane removes email addresses, phone numbers and text that looks like passwords, keys or card numbers. Copilot only suggests: nothing it produces is saved, applied or sent until you review and confirm it. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, these inputs and outputs are not used to train its models and are deleted within 30 days, unless longer retention is needed to enforce its usage policies or to comply with the law. In Demo Mode the sample Copilot runs entirely on your device with fictional data.

Support assistant

The Help & Support assistant (in the app and on our support website) answers questions from Launchlane's help content. It asks before you start, and the messages you type are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, PBC, to generate answers — email addresses, phone numbers and credential-like text are removed first. Launchlane does not store the conversation. To prevent abuse we keep a one-way hash of your network address and the time of each request for up to 24 hours. If you choose to email us, we receive your email and anything you include.

Service providers

  • Supabase — hosting, authentication, database and server functions.
  • Apple — App Store billing and subscription management.
  • Anthropic, PBC — AI processing, only when you use Copilot or the support assistant.

We share data with these providers only to run Launchlane, and each is bound by terms that require it to protect your data with the same or equal protection as described in this policy.

Sharing

Members of a workspace can see that workspace's content. We don't sell personal data or customer content. We may disclose information if required by law.

Customer notes are drafts

Launchlane can help you draft a note to a customer. Drafts are labelled for human review and are never sent by the app — Launchlane has no messaging capability. Anything you send, you send yourself with your own tools.

Retention and deletion

  • Delete your account in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account, after confirming with a fresh code (or your password). This permanently deletes your login and account data, any workspace where you are the only member (with all of its records and subscription link), and your Copilot usage records, then clears this device.
  • Shared workspaces are kept for their remaining members; records you created stay in the workspace without your account attached. If you own a workspace that has other members, ownership must be transferred before your account can be deleted — contact launchlaneapp@gmail.com until in-app transfer is available.
  • Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription — cancel it in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Demo data can be reset at any time in Settings.
  • Backups are kept for a limited period for disaster recovery and are then deleted.

Security

Access is enforced on the server by row-level security, and subscriptions are verified with Apple on the server. Sessions are stored in the iOS Keychain and traffic is encrypted in transit. No system is risk-free; use a device passcode and keep iOS up to date.

Children

Launchlane is a business tool for adults and is not directed to children.

International users

Your data may be processed in the United States and other countries where the providers above operate. We rely on their contractual safeguards when data is transferred across borders.

Changes

If we change the app in a way that affects privacy, we'll update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
